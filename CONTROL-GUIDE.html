Five visible markers, then paging. Shift + pad 1 holds a straight 1/32-bar slip loop. Other shifted pads are unassigned.

Clip → documented routes only

PLAY, CUE, Grid Start, hot cues, pitch and phase are translated to AP’s Clip routes. Hot cues are taps, not held previews.

Clip looping exists in AP, but the published Clip/Loop route means restart at end. It is not substituted for the track loop control. Unsupported slip/jump/formant/speed controls remain blocked.

Slip loops → one owner per deck

  1. Tap D or T to select timing; tap the active one again for straight.
  2. Keep holding pad A; press and hold pad B to hear B’s repeat.
  3. Release B while still holding A to return to A’s repeat.
  4. Release the final length: stop loop; keep the timing selected.

Timing starts straight and stays selected per deck. D/T changes during a hold retrigger the active repeat. Hot Cue’s 1/32 shortcut temporarily forces straight. Mouse D/T edits are not tracked; graceful exit clears D/T. Resuming a loop does not promise to preserve its old region.

Deck selection → two sides, four decks

LEFT: DECK 1 ↔ 3
Left Shift + left LOAD
RIGHT: DECK 2 ↔ 4
Right Shift + right LOAD

Hold that side’s Shift, tap LOAD, then release Shift. The sides switch independently; startup/restart selects 1 / 2.

  • Check the console/title for the selected deck. Mode lights show the bank, not the deck number.
  • Controls follow selection: transport, pads, main mixer channel, headphone CUE, LEDs/meter and ordinary LOAD.
  • Other decks keep playing. Selection does not load, stop, sync or take master. Each deck retains its bank and repeat timing.
  • Pickup re-arms: mixer and tempo controls wait until hardware reaches AP’s reported position. Missing feedback blocks pickup.

Shift + mode selects a bank, not a deck. In AP: four-deck view; FLX4 MIDI input off; OSC input 9090; feedback 127.0.0.1:9096, 30 Hz, Toggle All checked. See README for setup.

Known boundaries: routes may require visible controls in AP. No jog/scratch, crossfader, FX, learned cue-preview mappings or arrangement-internal mixing. Stop/restart after an AP restart or uncertain state. Ctrl+C attempts graceful releases; UDP loss and force-kills cannot guarantee them.
